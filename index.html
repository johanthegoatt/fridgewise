    Where the keep times come from

    The days each food keeps are taken from the US FoodSafety.gov Cold Food Storage Chart and the USDA FoodKeeper, using the shorter end of each range so the date leans safe. They are a guide, not a promise. A pack's own use-by date wins, and if something looks or smells off, bin it.
